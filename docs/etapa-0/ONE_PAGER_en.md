**LUKASH — Own your rise.**
The finances of the future, available today for all of Latin America.

## The problem
More than 200M Latin Americans are locked out of the financial infrastructure the institutional world already
uses (RWA, blockchain, yield on hard assets). It is not a lack of money: it is a lack of **access, trust,
and transparency**. The products that exist are opaque, expensive, or speak a language nobody under 45 understands.

## The solution — An Optimized Central Bank (ADR-028)
**$LUKA**, a deflationary token on Solana backed by the **Vault KASH Core**: a growing reserve of
hard assets (BTC, SOL, LST, USDC), 100% on-chain and **auditable in real time**. Every transaction in the ecosystem
feeds the Vault, burns supply, and rewards participants.

Unlike traditional central banks that issue currency against sovereign debt (inflationary by
design), LUKASH builds its reserve with real transactions and deflates the supply down to a fixed floor (3.3B).
The result: a transactional currency with a verifiable price floor (P_KASH = Vault / Supply), auditable 24/7.
It is not an alternative bank: it is **democratized access to the same infrastructure** that institutions are
already building — with an identity and progression layer that people actually want to use.

## How it works (the atom)
1. **Universal fee engine** — every operation distributes 35/35/15/15 → Vault / Burn-LP / O&M / Staking. Atomic, on-chain.
2. **Vault KASH Core** — auditable hard reserve that only grows; never liquidatable. Determines the "KASH Price" (value floor).
3. **Deflation with throttle** — supply 10B → 3.3B; the burn accelerates or slows based on market health (KASH Shield: circuit breakers, exit fee, anti-whale).
4. **Aura** — on-chain financial reputation, non-transferable. You level up by **participating**: Cachorro → Rastreador → Cazador → Alfa → Emperador → Shamán → **Titán**.
