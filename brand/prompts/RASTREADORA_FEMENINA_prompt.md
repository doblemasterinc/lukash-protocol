# Prompt — Rastreadora Femenina (Nivel 2: Aura 500–1,499)

> Adolescente/joven adulta. Ágil y observadora — ya no es una cachorra curiosa, es una exploradora.
> Estilo consistente con Cachorra (animated-realistic). Gradiente de color ligeramente más oscuro.
> SIN armadura, SIN accesorios. Primera aparición de bioluminiscencia sutil.
> Fecha: 2026-09-02

---

## PROMPT PRINCIPAL (copiar tal cual)

```
A young anthropomorphic female jaguar standing in a mystical jungle temple environment. Full body portrait, viewed from the front with a slight 3/4 angle, 3/4 body framing (head to knees visible). Photorealistic 3D render, cinematic quality. FEMALE character.

THE CHARACTER — BODY:
A female jaguar in late adolescence — approximately equivalent to a 16-18 year old human. Anthropomorphic: standing upright on two legs with humanoid body structure, fully covered in jaguar fur. The proportions are transitioning from juvenile to adult — the head is almost proportional now. She is slender, lithe, and agile — a dancer's or gymnast's build. Longer legs than the male rastreador relative to torso (built for speed and climbing). Narrow waist, lean arms with visible but understated definition. She's clearly faster than the male — everything about her build says velocity and precision. Visible long tail with spotted pattern, held in a graceful mid-height S-curve — alert but controlled, like a cat walking a narrow beam.

THE CHARACTER — HEAD AND FACE:
Classic jaguar head with warm honey-gold fur (#D9AA48 to #CBA040) and dark rosette spot pattern. The face has lost most of the baby roundness — higher cheekbones are now clearly defined, the muzzle is refined and tapered. Almond-shaped golden-amber eyes (#C9A84C to #E8C96A) with a sharp, analytical quality. FIRST BIOLUMINESCENCE: her eyes have a faint warm glow, but with a slightly different quality than the male — where his glow is ember-like (concentrated), hers has faint flecks of green (#00FF88 at ~5% opacity) mixed in the golden iris, like the jungle is already speaking to her and she's listening. She's looking directly at the viewer with a quiet confidence — not challenging, but assessing. Ears angled slightly outward and back — the posture of a cat who hears everything but doesn't react impulsively. Expression: composed intelligence, a slight knowing look, lips relaxed.

THE CHARACTER — FUR AND MARKINGS:
Warm honey-gold base coat (#D9AA48 to #CBA040), slightly warmer than the male. Classic jaguar rosettes. The fur lies close and sleek — even sleeker than the cachorra, developing the streamlined adult coat. FIRST SUBTLE MARKING: rather than the male's shoulder rosettes, her bioluminescent hints appear along the edges of her ears — the very tips of both ears have the faintest green luminous edge (#00FF88 at ~8% opacity), as if the energy enters through her hearing first (she's the one who listens and finds). This must be VERY subtle. The belly and chest fur is lighter cream. No circuit patterns, no tech lines.

THE CHARACTER — CLOTHING:
MINIMAL but slightly more considered than the cachorra. A fitted dark wrap/bandeau across the upper chest (#1A1A1E to #2A2A2E), more tailored than the cachorra's — same humble materials but she's figured out how to make them work better. A short asymmetric wrap around the waist, cut higher on one side for freedom of movement — practical for climbing and running. Braided cord belt with a small pouch (smaller than the male's — she travels lighter). Bare arms, bare midriff, bare feet (paw-feet). A thin braided cord anklet on one ankle — functional (she can use it to mark a trail or tie something), with a single small knot that serves as a distance marker she's counting. Optional: the jungle-fiber braid behind her ear from the cachorra phase is still there, slightly longer now — a touch of continuity.

THE CHARACTER — POSTURE:
She's standing on a narrow stone ledge or raised platform — demonstrating her agility and comfort with heights. One foot is placed precisely on the edge of the stone, the other slightly behind, weight centered. She's perfectly balanced. One hand rests lightly on a hanging vine to her side (not gripping it — just touching it, as if reading its vibration). The other hand hangs relaxed at her side, fingers slightly curled. Her body is angled slightly to show the 3/4 view, giving depth. Unlike the male's active crouch, her posture says "I already found what I'm looking for — I'm deciding what to do about it."

THE ENVIRONMENT:
Same temple corridor area as the male rastreador — maintaining world consistency. But she's higher up. She's standing on a raised stone shelf or partially collapsed wall section, about 1 meter above the corridor floor. This puts her at a vantage point — she surveys from above. The same Mayan carved stone walls with moss and roots. A LUKASH isotipo carving (stylized feline head with infinity symbol — upload reference: brand/logos/lukash protocol isotipo.png) is visible on the wall near her elevated position, partially lit by the first traces of green light in its grooves. Hanging vines (one of which she's touching). Dense jungle visible through gaps. Bioluminescent particles at the same density as the male's scene. Below her, the mossy stone floor of the corridor is visible, giving a sense of height/depth. Ferns and vegetation growing from the wall near her feet.

LIGHTING:
Same slightly darker mood as the male rastreador — enclosed corridor, filtered light from ceiling gaps. But her elevated position catches slightly more light — one shaft of filtered daylight illuminates her upper body and face while her legs are in slightly deeper shadow. The bioluminescent particles float at the level of her waist and below, giving a sense of her height above them. Overall color grading matches the male: 10% darker than cachorra, slightly desaturated, deepening warmth. The light catching her honey-gold fur creates a subtle warmth differentiation from the male's cooler tan.

MOOD AND NARRATIVE:
This image communicates: "I see everything from up here. I've already mapped the route." Where the male rastreador is learning to track by getting close to the ground, she's learning by getting above it — different strategy, same goal. Her intelligence is spatial, strategic. She doesn't need to crouch — she found the vantage point. The slight smile says she's one step ahead.

TECHNICAL:
Photorealistic 3D render with animated-realistic quality matching the cachorra and male rastreador. Portrait orientation (3:4 or 9:16). High detail. Cinematic depth of field. Color grading: slightly darker than cachorra, slightly warmer overall tone than the male rastreador. Same "late afternoon" feeling.

DO NOT INCLUDE: Any text, UI elements, holographic screens, watermarks. No armor, tech suits, weapons. No intense bioluminescence — only the first hints (ear tips, eye flecks). No sexualized proportions — athletic adolescent, not adult. No other characters. No cartoon style.
```

## ADDON: ENTORNO BIOLUMINISCENTE (probar por separado)

> Pegar al final del prompt principal para activar el efecto neón en el entorno.
> Mismo addon que el masculino — se comparte para mantener consistencia de mundo.

```
ENVIRONMENT ENHANCEMENT — BIOLUMINESCENT FOLIAGE: Some of the fern fronds and hanging vine leaves nearest to the character have their edges faintly outlined with a very subtle green bioluminescent glow (#00FF88 at 15% opacity) — as if the jungle itself is alive with energy and the deeper you go, the more it reveals. This effect is ONLY on 4-5 leaves nearest to the character, not the entire jungle. The glow follows the natural leaf edge contour — organic, not neon signage. Additionally, one or two thin root/vine tendrils on the stone wall near her elevated position pulse with a faint golden light (#C9A84C at 10% opacity), like veins of energy running through the temple. These effects must be extremely subtle — atmospheric, not overwhelming.
```
