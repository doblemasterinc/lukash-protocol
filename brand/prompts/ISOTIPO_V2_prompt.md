## PROMPT PRINCIPAL (copiar tal cual)

```
A three-dimensional sacred emblem centered in the frame, viewed from the front. The emblem depicts a stylized big cat head (jaguar/panther hybrid) constructed from geometric low-polygon wireframe segments rendered as physical metallic gold tubing and structural beams.

THE FELINE HEAD: Frontal view, bilateral symmetry. The face is built from triangular geometric facets — like a low-poly 3D model made physical. Each edge of the polygon mesh is a gold metallic tube/beam (#C9A84C with realistic metallic reflections and highlights up to #E8C96A). Two pointed ears formed by triangular facets. The forehead has a prominent geometric chevron. Eyes are angular, glowing bright green (#00FF88) from within — like energy contained inside the wireframe structure. Visible whisker lines rendered as thin gold wires extending from the muzzle sides. The nose bridge is formed by converging triangular planes. The overall head has more geometric detail and definition than a simple outline — you can see the individual polygon faces as flat planes between the gold edges, filled with very dark matte material (#0A0A0C).

THE INFINITY SYMBOL: Below the jaw, the infinity symbol (∞) is rendered as thick 3D gold metallic tubing — like industrial-grade pipes or conduits. The tubes have visible joins, bolts, and subtle mechanical details. They catch light realistically. The gold transitions to having green edge-lighting (#00FF88) along the inner curves of the infinity loops, as if green energy flows through the tubes. The tubing connects seamlessly to the jaw structure of the feline head.

THE VAULT VORTEX: At the exact crossover point of the infinity symbol, instead of a simple circle, there is a green spiral vortex — a swirling portal of bright green energy (#00FF88 core, darker green #3DAA6B edges). It looks like a miniature wormhole or energy vortex spinning slowly. The vortex emits green light that illuminates the surrounding gold tubing from below. This is the visual representation of the Vault (La Bóveda) — a portal of perpetual energy.

BACKGROUND: Very dark environment. The background texture is rough dark stone/mineral/obsidian rock with visible cracks and irregular surface (#0A0A0C to #0F0F12). Embedded in the rock surface, thin green circuit traces (#00FF88 at ~30% opacity) run in geometric patterns — straight lines with right angles, resembling PCB traces carved into ancient stone. The combination creates a "ancient technology buried in rock" aesthetic — primal meets digital.

LIGHTING: Dramatic low-key lighting. Primary light source is the green vortex at center, casting green light upward onto the gold structure. Secondary ambient light creates subtle gold highlights on the metallic tubing edges. The green glow from the eyes adds two point lights on the upper portion. Overall mood: dark, powerful, precious — like discovering a technological artifact in an underground temple.

STYLE: Photorealistic 3D render. The gold must look like real brushed/polished gold metal — with reflections, highlights, and subtle surface imperfections. The rock background must have realistic texture. The green energy (eyes, vortex, circuit traces) should have a bioluminescent quality — organic light, not neon signage. The overall aesthetic is "sacred technological artifact" — something between a Mayan relic and a sci-fi power core.

RENDERING: High-resolution, 1:1 square format. Depth of field with the emblem in sharp focus and background edges slightly softer. The emblem occupies approximately 80% of the canvas. Cinematic quality.

DO NOT INCLUDE: Any text, watermarks, or signatures. No human figures. No additional objects beyond the emblem and background. No cartoon or anime style — this must look like a premium 3D render or concept art for a AAA game/film.
```

- "gold metallic tubing (#C9A84C)" → el material de la tabla
- "green (#00FF88)" → el color energía de la tabla
- "green spiral vortex" → el tamaño/velocidad del vórtice de la tabla
