```
A complete brand lockup centered on a pure black background (#0A0A0C), composed of a sacred emblem above and text below, perfectly centered and vertically stacked.

UPPER ELEMENT — THE EMBLEM (ISOTIPO):
A perfectly symmetrical stylized big cat head (jaguar/panther hybrid) rendered in clean, continuous golden line art (#C9A84C) with consistent stroke weight. Frontal view. Two pointed triangular ears with inner detail lines. Hollow almond-shaped eyes (outlines only, no pupils). Broad nose bridge narrowing to a small triangular nose. Below the jaw, an infinity symbol (∞) flows organically from the chin line — smooth rounded loops. At the infinity crossover point, a double concentric circle (vault symbol). Behind the entire golden emblem, a soft ethereal green glow (#00FF88 to #3DAA6B) forming a subtle diamond-shaped radiance with small 4-pointed sparkle stars at the cardinal points. The glow fades to transparent at the edges.

LOWER ELEMENT — THE WORDMARK:
Below the emblem, with comfortable spacing (approximately 15-20% of the emblem height as gap):

Line 1: "LUKASH" in a serif display typeface similar to Cinzel Bold. The letters are large, bold, with wide letter-spacing (tracking ~0.25em). The "L", "U", "K", "A", "S", "H" are all uppercase. The text color is primarily warm off-white/ivory (#F0EAD6) BUT the letter "K" is distinctly colored in gold (#C9A84C) — creating a visual anchor at the center of the word. The entire word has a very subtle green text-shadow/glow (barely visible, just enough to connect it to the emblem's green aura above). The typeface has elegant serifs — classical, not modern/geometric. Think Roman inscriptions meets luxury branding.

Line 2: "Protocol" in the same serif typeface but lighter weight, smaller size (approximately 45% of the "LUKASH" text height), centered below "LUKASH" with tight spacing. Color: soft green (#33FFAA to #5FCA8E). All lowercase or title case. This line has a subtle green glow matching the emblem's aura.

BACKGROUND: Pure black (#0A0A0C). Very subtle circuit board trace lines in barely-visible dark gray (#1A1A1E) extend horizontally from both sides behind the composition, with 90-degree turns resembling PCB traces. These must be extremely subtle — nearly invisible, adding depth without distraction.

OVERALL COMPOSITION: Vertical stack — emblem on top, "LUKASH" in the middle, "Protocol" at the bottom. All three elements share the same vertical center axis. The total composition has a 16:9 or 2:1 horizontal aspect ratio (wider than tall), with the emblem and text centered in the frame. The composition feels like a premium fintech logo — sacred, technological, authoritative.

STYLE: The emblem is flat 2D line art (no 3D, no metallic shading on the lines). The text is clean and sharp. The ONLY depth effects are the green glows and text shadows. Ultra-clean edges throughout. This should look like a professional brand identity mark, not an illustration.

DO NOT INCLUDE: Any additional graphics, decorative elements, taglines, or watermarks beyond what is described. The "K" in LUKASH must be gold — this is a key brand element. Do not add gradients to the gold emblem lines. Do not make this 3D or photorealistic — it must remain flat/graphic.
```
